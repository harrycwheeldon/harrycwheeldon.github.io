The Starting Point

Understanding
the Challenge

21 acquisitions. Multiple systems. Inconsistent processes. Increasing pressure on Finance to deliver timely, accurate information. Before designing the solution, we need to be honest about what we're dealing with.

⏱️

Time is the real constraint

When month-end takes a full month, the team has no capacity left for transformation. Every initiative competes for the same stretched resource. Before we can fix anything, we need to buy back time.

🔀

Inconsistency across entities

Rapid growth through acquisition means multiple different ways of doing things — different systems, different standards, different levels of rigour. Inconsistency creates ambiguity, and ambiguity slows everything down.

📊

Finance is reactive, not proactive

SLT and the PE house are asking questions Finance can't answer quickly. Without live visibility, Finance spends time fielding queries rather than driving insight. That needs to change.

Impact vs Effort

The Approach:
Three Tiers

Prioritised by impact relative to the time they cost to implement. Each tier unlocks the next. Click each tier to explore the detail.

Tier 1

Buy Back the Time

Month 1 – 2  ·  Quick wins, highest impact per hour invested
▼
Programme Actions
  • Accounting policy memo: set materiality thresholds and resolve key judgement calls. Fewer debates = faster close.
  • Audit every recurring report — kill anything nobody actually uses. Free time is transformation time.
  • Hard data deadlines with named ownership across the business. Finance stops waiting; the business delivers.
  • Identify the critical path of month-end — what is the single bottleneck holding everything else up?
My First Actions
  • Month 1: shadow a full month-end cycle end to end — if month-end takes a month, understanding it properly takes a month too. Map every handoff, every wait, every bottleneck before touching anything.
  • Sit with each team member individually — they know where they get stuck and what's wasteful. That insight is gold.
  • Set up a simple RAG transformation tracker, visible to Vicki, Martyn, and Bobby. Progress shouldn't require chasing.
Tier 2

Automate the Repetitive

Month 2 – 4  ·  Target high-volume, low-value tasks
▼
Programme Actions
  • Identify the highest-volume, lowest-value tasks across the function — journals prepared manually across multiple entities, for example, are a strong candidate for automation where volume justifies it.
  • Where repetitive reporting exists, explore whether exception-based flagging could replace manual review — surfacing only the variances that need attention rather than reviewing everything.
  • Begin building live dashboards for SLT and the PE house — reducing inbound queries frees the team to focus on insight.
  • Structured process audit: categorise every task by value vs repetition. Anything low-value and repetitive is an automation candidate.
My First Actions
  • Run AI upskilling sessions with the team — they know their roles best and will identify their own quick wins once they have the tools.
  • Introduce agentic AI where appropriate: tasks that are rule-based and repeatable should be delegated to automation, not people.
  • Ensure NetSuite integration is central to automation design — no point automating into a system we're replacing.
Tier 3

Systems & Structure

Month 4 – 6  ·  High impact, high effort — requires capacity built in Tiers 1 & 2
▼
Programme Actions
  • NetSuite rollout already underway — critical focus is ensuring all existing systems feed into it cleanly, eliminating manual re-entry.
  • Review team structure for fit with the future operating model — consider a revenue/costs split rather than per-entity ownership.
  • Standardise chart of accounts and reporting frameworks across all acquired entities.
  • Build the finance function that can support the next phase of M&A — scalable, automated, and audit-ready.
My First Actions
  • Get fully up to speed on where the NetSuite implementation stands — understand integration gaps and position myself as the bridge between finance operations and technical delivery.
  • Map which legacy systems must remain and ensure clean data flows are designed, not manual workarounds.
  • Contribute to the structural review with data from the process audit — evidence-led recommendations, not assumptions.

Why This Works

The Goal:
Month-End in Under a Week

This isn't theory. The same approach — process audit, automation, AI adoption — took Equifax UK's month-end from 5 days finishing at 10pm to 2 days finishing at 5pm. The same discipline, applied here, gets Progeny to under a week.
